# Acceptable Use Policy

**Product:** PayChat · **Operator:** Braincade Holdings (Pty) Ltd
**Version:** 1.0-draft · **Legal review status: DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED BY COUNSEL**
**Last updated:** 2026-09-19

This policy forms part of the [`TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md`](TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md).
Breach may result in suspension or termination of access and referral to the
relevant authorities.

---

## 1. You must use PayChat lawfully

You must not use PayChat to:

1. commit fraud, obtain funds or services dishonestly, or use a payment method
   you are not authorised to use;
2. launder money, finance terrorism, evade sanctions, or process proceeds of
   crime;
3. carry out any activity prohibited by Botswana law or by the law that applies
   where you or the recipient are located;
4. infringe intellectual property, privacy or other rights;
5. impersonate any person or misrepresent your affiliation or identity.

## 2. Prohibited transactions

You must not use PayChat to pay for or receive payment for:

- illegal goods, substances or services;
- unlicensed gambling or betting where a licence is required and not held;
- unlicensed financial, investment or lending services;
- weapons, ammunition or controlled goods without a lawful permit;
- counterfeit goods or stolen property;
- sexual services, or any content involving the exploitation of children;
- unregistered pharmaceuticals or controlled substances;
- Ponzi, pyramid or other fraudulent investment schemes;
- cryptocurrency or virtual-asset trading where prohibited by the applicable
  Payment Partner;
- any goods or services a Payment Partner prohibits under its own rules.

Absent a Payment Partner rule to the contrary, this list applies from the first
use of the Service.

## 3. Prohibited technical conduct

You must not:

1. probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service without written
   authorisation;
2. bypass, disable or circumvent authentication, authorisation, rate limiting,
   risk controls, or payment confirmation steps;
3. access data belonging to another user, or attempt to;
4. tamper with, forge, or replay messages, transactions or webhooks;
5. introduce malware or any code intended to harm the Service or its users;
6. scrape, harvest or bulk-extract data without written permission;
7. use the Service to send unsolicited bulk messages or spam;
8. reverse engineer any part of the Service except as permitted by law;
9. use automated means to create accounts or to inflate activity;
10. interfere with the availability of the Service for others.

## 4. Responsible disclosure

Security researchers acting in good faith are welcome to report vulnerabilities
to `security@paychat.bw`. Do not exploit a finding beyond what is needed to
demonstrate it, do not access other users' data, and do not disclose publicly
before a fix is available. We will acknowledge receipt and work with you on
timelines. **A formal bug-bounty programme is not offered at this time.**

## 5. Merchant and business users

If you accept payments through PayChat you additionally must not:

1. misrepresent the goods or services you sell;
2. use a business name that is not yours or that misleads customers;
3. bill for goods or services you do not intend to provide;
4. process payments on behalf of an undisclosed third party (payment
   aggregation) without our written agreement;
5. store payment credentials you have no right to hold.

## 6. Monitoring and enforcement

We may use automated checks (rate limits, velocity rules, risk scoring) and
manual review to detect prohibited use. We may:

1. decline or hold a transaction pending review;
2. require additional verification;
3. suspend or terminate access;
4. retain records of the conduct as required by law;
5. report suspected unlawful activity to the relevant authority.

Where lawful and practicable, we will tell you that action has been taken and
how to raise a query.

## 7. Consequences

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition: warning → transaction
decline → account restriction → account termination → referral to authorities.
Serious cases may be actioned immediately without prior warning.

## 8. Appeals

Appeal a decision at `support@paychat.bw` with your account reference and any
supporting information. See
[`SUPPORT_AND_DISPUTES.md`](SUPPORT_AND_DISPUTES.md).
