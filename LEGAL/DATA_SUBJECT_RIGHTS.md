# Data Subject Rights Procedure

**Product:** PayChat · **Controller:** Braincade Holdings (Pty) Ltd
**Version:** 1.0-draft · **Legal review status: DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED BY COUNSEL**
**Last updated:** 2026-09-19

How a data subject (a PayChat user) exercises their rights under the Botswana
Data Protection Act, 2018, and how Braincade handles each request.

---

## 1. Request channel

`privacy@paychat.bw` — or in-app: **Settings → Privacy → My data**. Requests may
also be sent to the registered address on the Terms and Conditions.

## 2. Requests we accept

| Right | What it means | How PayChat responds |
|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Obtain a copy of personal data held about you | Structured export of profile, transactions and account data; in-app self-service where available, otherwise provided by support |
| **Rectification** | Correct inaccurate data | Profile fields are editable in-app; data held only by a Payment Partner is referred to that partner |
| **Erasure** | Request deletion | Deletion of what we may lawfully delete; retained financial/audit records explained with the reason and the retention period |
| **Restriction / objection** | Ask us to stop or limit certain processing | Processing stopped where no legal or contractual obligation requires it; the request and decision are recorded |
| **Portability** | Receive data in a structured, machine-readable form | JSON export covering account and transaction data |
| **Withdraw consent** | Withdraw consent given at registration | Consent-based processing ceases; contract-performance and legal-obligation processing continues as permitted |
| **Complaint** | Escalate to the regulator | We explain how to contact the Information and Data Protection Commission |

## 3. Procedure

### 3.1 Intake
1. Log the request with a unique reference in the privacy register.
2. Record: requester, right invoked, date received, channel.

### 3.2 Identity verification
We must not disclose or delete data on an unverified request.
- The requester must be authenticated as the account holder (signed-in
  self-service, or a verified support session), **or**
- provide sufficient evidence of identity and control of the registered phone
  number.

### 3.3 Assessment
1. Locate all personal data for the requester using the account identifier.
2. Identify any legal, regulatory, AML or Payment Partner obligation that limits
   the request.
3. Confirm no third party's rights would be infringed by disclosure.

### 3.4 Response
1. Provide the outcome in a clear, plain-language form.
2. Where a request is refused in whole or in part, state the ground for refusal
   and how to challenge it.
3. Record the decision, the reason, the date and the responder.

### 3.5 Timelines
| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge receipt | 3 business days |
| Complete straightforward request | 30 calendar days |
| Complex request | 30 days, with a written explanation and a revised date |

**These timelines must be validated against the Act's statutory periods by
counsel before publication.**

## 4. Fees

No fee is charged for the first request in a 12-month period. A reasonable,
documented administrative fee may be charged for manifestly unfounded or
excessive repeat requests; the fee basis must be confirmed by counsel.

## 5. Security of responses

- Data is delivered only through channels the requester has authenticated.
- Exports are delivered as files with short-lived access, not as inline personal
  data in unsolicited e-mail.
- The delivery event is recorded in the audit log.

## 6. Records

The privacy register records: reference, right, dates, verification method,
decision, refusal grounds, delivery method and reviewer. The register is
classified **C2 Internal** and is reviewed at least quarterly.

## 7. Self-service coverage in the product

| Capability | Status |
|---|---|
| View own profile and sessions | Implemented |
| Revoke a session | Implemented |
| View own transaction history | Implemented |
| Export own data | **Partial** — support-assisted; full self-service export is a roadmap item |
| Self-service account deletion | **Not implemented** — handled by support |
| Correction of profile details | Implemented |

## 8. Escalation

Unresolved requests are escalated to the responsible manager and, if still
unresolved, to the Information and Data Protection Commission. **The Commission's
submission process must be confirmed by counsel.**
